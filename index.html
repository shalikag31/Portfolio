EduProof AI is a citation-grounded educational assistant designed to provide answers backed by uploaded educational sources rather than unsupported generated responses.

  • PDF/text document ingestion
  • Upload educational materials
  • Demo knowledge base
  • Vector indexing with FAISS
  • Source-grounded question answering
  • Citation-backed answers with retrieved chunks
  • Exact source quote display
  • Insufficient-evidence behavior

Web Application

ExamSphere

ExamSphere is an online examination system designed around a real classroom workflow, allowing students to take subject-based MCQ examinations while administrators manage exams, question banks and results.

  • Student registration & login
  • Administrator login
  • Subject-wise, timed MCQ examinations
  • Automatic submission when time expires
  • Question palette navigation & review marking
  • Instant pass/fail result calculation
  • Exam history & admin question-bank management
  • Data stored locally in the browser (localStorage)
